# The letters between rulers (src/diplomacy.c), in the formulas of the
# Amarna and Ugarit letters (vision.md section 3). Every letter gets the
# same arguments: {arg 0} the ruler written to and {arg 1} their title,
# {arg 2} the ruler writing and {arg 3} their title, {arg 4} the writer's
# people. The texts from letter_war.txt on must stay in the Makefile's
# order, which is LETTER_* in src/diplomacy.c. A popup holds 400
# characters, the names included.
#
# This one is the first, when a people is met.
You have met the {arg 4}.

Say to {arg 0}, {arg 1}, my brother: thus says {arg 2}, {arg 3}, your \
brother. For me all goes well. For you, your household, your chariots \
and your land, may all go well. Let there be peace between our lands.
